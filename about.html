About

We started OmniMark because we kept getting hired to fix the same thing.

Every audit ended the same way: the marketing was fine, the sales team was fine, and the space between them was a mess. Agencies wouldn't touch it because it isn't a channel, and consultancies wrote decks about it without building anything. So we built the team that does both.
